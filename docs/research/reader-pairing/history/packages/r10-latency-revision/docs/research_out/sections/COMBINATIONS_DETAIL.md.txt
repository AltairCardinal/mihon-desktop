## C01 共享物化×证据旁路（Q015,Q033）

- 状态/裁定：**completed / probe_first**；证据：同一模型、每设计0/A/B/AB消融（模型单位）
- 干预：A=一个物理物化服务两种消费者（否则可见与证据各物化一次）；B=可见路径不等证书先物化
- 联合设计：I=消费者主导：成员/可见工作必需，邻居证据投机；II=中性物化服务：按对象位置FIFO，按消费者发租约
- 设计I：consumer-led: members/visible required, neighbour evidence speculative
- 设计II：neutral materialisation service: FIFO by object position, leases per consumer
- 机制差异：priority by consumer (I) vs consumer-blind service order with lease delivery (II)
- 标量f：complete-unit ack time (model units)（越低越好）

## C02 共享工作×多消费者取消（Q029,Q063）

- 状态/裁定：**completed / engineering_trial**；证据：同一模型、每设计0/A/B/AB消融（模型单位）
- 干预：A=去重：C2挂到C1的作业上（否则C2自建作业）；B=取消/放弃协议
- 联合设计：I=引用计数租约，计数归零才物理取消；II=需求集合+优先级继承（加入时作业取最强需求者优先级），放弃时显式所有权转移
- 设计I：reference-counted lease; physical cancel only when no live demand
- 设计II：demand set with priority inheritance; explicit ownership transfer on abandonment
- 机制差异：I keeps the creator's priority and only protects against cancellation; II changes the job's priority to the strongest live demander at join and at transfer
- 标量f：C2 result time (model units)（越低越好）

## C04 升级协议×字节/时限许可（Q027,Q040）

- 状态/裁定：**completed / engineering_trial**；证据：同一模型、每设计0/A/B/AB消融（模型单位）
- 干预：A=原位升级（否则取消重启T'）；B=许可结算方式
- 联合设计：I=保留任务与许可、只提高消费者优先级；许可由唯一账户在真实退出时结算（关闭context不退款）。II=升级时把任务所有权转给新context并原子迁移预留
- 设计I：keep task and permit; raise consumer priority; settle once at the real exit
- 设计II：transfer ownership to the new context and migrate the reservation atomically
- 机制差异：I leaves the permit with the creator and forbids refund-before-exit; II moves the permit to the new owner
- 标量f：interleavings (of 24) with a ledger violation (hole, double refund, unrenewable permit)（越低越好）
